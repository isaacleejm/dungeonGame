**BACKSTORY:**

In a world filled with wizards, magic is not bound by rigid rules. Any wizard can learn and cast any spell they encounter. However, every wizard is born with a **Destined Spell**—a unique spell that resonates with them more deeply than any other. A wizard can master countless spells, but their Destined Spell is the one they can wield with unmatched power, precision, and efficiency. No other wizard, no matter how skilled, can use that particular spell as well as its destined wielder.

The main character, **Zardius**, is a young wizard who has recently been nominated for entry into the world's most prestigious group of mages: the **Top Wizard Cohort**.

Being accepted into the cohort is no small achievement. A wizard must have accomplished extraordinary feats with their Destined Spell before they can even be considered.

Zardius's Destined Spell is the **Mirror Spell**. He can summon up to ten magical mirrors and use them to redirect and concentrate energy into powerful beams. Through his adventures, Zardius has saved hundreds of civilians from a dangerous local terror.

Yet, despite his accomplishments, Zardius feels like he doesn't belong among the world's greatest wizards.

There is one wizard in particular whom he admires above everyone else: **Zane, the Sword Wizard**.

Zane's Destined Spell is the **Sword Spell**, allowing him to summon up to twenty magical swords at once and launch ten of them simultaneously with incredible speed and precision. Zane has used his power to save thousands of civilians from a monstrous threat that once terrorised entire regions.

Zardius was one of those civilians.

To Zardius, Zane is everything a wizard should aspire to be. Compared to someone who had saved thousands, Zardius struggles to see how saving hundreds could possibly be enough to deserve a place beside him.

After a meeting with the cohort one day, Zardius wanders into a library. While searching through the shelves, he discovers something unexpected: a book written by Zane himself.

Zardius immediately picks it up.

The book recounts Zane's adventures, the battles he fought, the people he saved, and the spells he developed throughout his journey. But one spell appears again and again—the Sword Spell, Zane's Destined Spell.

Zardius becomes completely absorbed in the stories.

Then—

**RUMBLE.**

A strange vibration shakes the library.

Zardius looks around.

Before he can react, the floor beneath him tears open. A massive black hole suddenly forms beneath his feet, dragging everything around him into its depths.

The book flies from his hands.

Zardius reaches for it.

Then everything goes black.

When he awakens, Zardius finds himself lying on cold stone inside an unfamiliar dungeon.

The library is gone.

The world he knows is gone.

Beside him lies the book he had been reading.

But there is something else.

A strange **crest** has appeared on the palm of his hand.

Unable to understand where he is or how he arrived there, Zardius begins exploring the dungeon in search of a way out. As he ventures deeper, he encounters strange creatures, dangerous traps, and remnants of an unfamiliar civilisation.

Then, during one of his battles, something impossible happens.

Zardius instinctively raises his hand.

A magical sword appears.

He freezes.

The sword disappears.

He tries again.

Another sword materialises.

It is not his Destined Spell.

It should not be possible for him to wield the Sword Spell with such power.

Yet somehow, Zardius has discovered that he can use **Zane's Sword Spell**.

And as he ventures deeper into this mysterious world, Zardius begins to realise that the strange crest on his hand, the book he brought with him, and his ability to wield a spell that was destined for another wizard may all be connected.

Perhaps being brought to this world was not an accident.

Perhaps Zardius was brought here for a reason.

And perhaps becoming a great wizard was never about being as powerful as Zane.

Perhaps it was about discovering what makes **Zardius** powerful in the first place.
